\begin{abstract}
We bring \emph{two-way} rigid--modal contact coupling into a real-time,
augmented-Lagrangian rigid-body solver. Each body's linear vibration modes are
carried as first-class compliant-constraint degrees of freedom: a single shared
unilateral contact multiplier both excites the modes of the bodies in contact and
reacts on their rigid frames, so linear and angular momentum are conserved by
construction and vibration propagates through a body--body contact network.
Because the host solver runs at a fixed, low iteration budget, this coupling can
inject spurious energy into the modes; we bound the contact$\to$modal transfer with
an enforced per-step passivity inequality $\dEmod \le \eta\,\dErig$ that keeps the
coupling stable at interactive rates.
\end{abstract}
