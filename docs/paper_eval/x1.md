# Stage X1 — Enforced passivity in the native path (foundation §15)

**Status:** mechanism complete + tested; robustness sweep pending final run.
**Code:** `dcr/avbd/_solver/passivity.py` (new), hooks in `solver_xpbd.py` and
`solver_6dof.py` (default OFF — behaviour-neutral). **Tests:**
`tests/avbd_native/test_passivity_clamp.py`. **Branch:** `stageX1-native-passivity`.

## Goal

Turn "passive by energy" from the X0 empirical safe region into a per-step
guarantee: the native modal support cannot gain more energy than the rigid bodies
lose (foundation §15, `ΔE_modal ≤ η·ΔE_rigid_loss`), enforced online.

## Mechanism

Per rigid substep, on the CPU host symplectic path (the only path the modal
support runs on — device+symplectic is unsupported upstream):

1. **Budget from irreversible contact loss.** `rigid_loss = grav_work − ΔKE` over
   the substep — the rigid kinetic energy the contact/velocity solve removed. It is
   **zero for free ballistic motion** (up or down: `ΔKE == grav_work`) and positive
   only when contact inelastically dissipates KE. This is the ONLY funding source,
   so a bouncing impactor cannot over-credit the budget with its reversible
   gravitational PE. (An earlier KE-only budget was too strict — it starved the
   grav-PE→elastic-PE transfer of settling objects; a full-mechanical budget was too
   loose — it counted the whole fall. `grav_work − ΔKE` is the correct middle.)
2. **Reservoir.** Losses accumulate in a reservoir (`PassivityLedger`); modal gains
   debit it. A per-substep budget would trip spuriously on free oscillation and
   delayed re-excitation — the reservoir makes the guarantee CUMULATIVE
   (`Σ modal_gain ≤ η·Σ rigid_loss`) while staying inert when slack remains.
3. **Full-state γ projection.** When the substep would overshoot the reservoir, the
   whole modal state `(q, q̇)` is scaled by `γ = √((E_m_old + budget)/E_m_new)`, so
   the modal-energy gain lands exactly on the ceiling. Scaling both q and q̇ bounds
   KE *and* PE (both quadratic) — a projection of the over-shot state onto the
   passive manifold. `γ = 1` (inert) whenever the ring already fits.

Two solvers, two roles (matching their measured behaviour, audit + X0):
- **XPBD — active clamp.** XPBD injects at low budget (up to 167× in the robustness
  matrix); the γ-projection enforces the bound.
- **AVBD — monitor-only.** AVBD is empirically passive (audit: passivity ≤ 0.73,
  never injects). Its energy transfers with a one-substep PE-leads-KE lag, so an
  active clamp would steal in-transit energy. It runs the ledger to CONFIRM
  passivity without perturbing the solver.

## Invariant checked

`PassivityLedger.passive()`: `max_t [E_modal(t) − E_modal(0) − η·Σloss(t)] ≤ η·max_substep_loss + tol`
— peak modal energy never exceeds initial + cumulative rigid loss, allowing a
one-substep in-transit lead (the accounting granularity). Robust to KE↔PE
oscillation and re-excitation double-counting (unlike a cumulative-sum metric).

- AVBD: monitor-mode confirms passivity (net excess 0.026 J = 0.2% of one substep's
  13.3 J throughput — a discretization artifact, not energy creation).

## Deviations (CLAUDE.md rule 3)

- The γ-projection scales the co-solved position `q` post-solve — a bounded
  correction to the support geometry that the next substep re-solves. Justified as
  projecting an under-converged, over-shot modal state onto the passive manifold
  (the support cannot store more energy than the contact delivered). Inert (`γ=1`)
  whenever the solver is well-converged, so the paper config never invokes it.
- Host-path only. The device q-block does not carry the clamp; enabling
  `_enforce_modal_passivity` on a CUDA run is a no-op there (the symplectic modal
  step is itself host-only, so the paper config is unaffected).
